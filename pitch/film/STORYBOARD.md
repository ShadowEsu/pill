# Pill · demo film storyboard

**Concept (one line):** one shape carries the whole story: the quarter tablet's cup becomes the amber NEEDS LAB pill, the pill becomes one in a row of ten, the row's dot becomes "$40", "$40" opens the navy station, the station's panel splits into the three verdict stamps, the NEEDS LAB stamp opens into the real app, the app shrinks into the escrow lock, the lock becomes the x402 `402` card, the cards become the honesty card's columns, the columns become the Figma workflow, and the workflow closes into the Pill mark.

Method (after prompt-motion's showreel): `film.html` is a pure function of time (`window.seek(t)`, `window.DURATION = 158`); critically damped springs and exponential ease-outs computed from `t`; no CSS animation, no timers, no state between frames. `render.mjs` screenshots 3 subframes per frame at 30 fps and blends them with `ffmpeg tmix=frames=3` (motion blur). The soundtrack is code (`window.renderAudio()`, OfflineAudioContext, 96 BPM, soft pad, sine bass, quiet pluck, a tick on every scene change). Voice-over is macOS `say -v Samantha -r 175` (no Ava/Zoe premium voice installed), one AIFF per line (`vo.mjs`); caption chunks are timed to the pauses in each AIFF.

Type: Instrument Serif (display, captions), Inter (body), JetBrains Mono (labels). Palette: paper #F7F8F7, ink #16181B, sky #4FB3FF, mint #5FD39A, amber #E09A3A, violet #7A5CFF (x402 only), navy #0E1216 (the station). Words in ink or white; colour only in fills, lines and chips.
