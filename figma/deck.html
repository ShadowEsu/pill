THE STATION
04 / 21
Pill · Cal Hacks 13.0 · pill-site.vercel.app
Lot numbers can be copied. Dissolution cannot.
A recall lookup trusts the label. Pill measures the tablet itself: one tablet in a cup of water inside a dark chamber, two light sensors watching it dissolve.
Arduino UNO Q · 3.3 V · 12-bit
Two photoresistors in 10 kΩ dividers from the 3V3 pin: A0 across the cup, A1 at 90° to the beam.
A dark reading every second
All LEDs off, both sensors read. Room light cancels out of the signal instead of leaking into it.
Blue, white, red in turn
Each LED lights alone and both sensors read again: light that gets through, light that scatters sideways.
One CSV line a second
ms, dark, blue, white, red for each sensor, at 115200 baud. The firmware decides nothing.

4. The station · notes: The station is an Arduino UNO Q, a 3.3-volt board with a 12-bit ADC. These are photos of it from Saturday: a purple 3D-printed chamber holding a clear cup of water, a side box with white, blue and red LEDs, and the UNO Q next to it. The drawing shows where the two photoresistors sit, one in line with the beam and one at ninety degrees. Once a second it turns every LED off and reads both, the dark reading that cancels room light, then lights blue, white and red in turn and reads again. It sends one CSV line a second. The firmware decides nothing; the laptop does the maths.

6. How a run works · notes: One whole tablet in a small cup, stirred by hand. Quick mode is sixty seconds after the tablet goes in; full mode runs two to ten minutes until the curve is flat. The run is compared with a reference, and the answer is one of three. A model explains the result in plain English but never sets it.

THREE ANSWERS
07 / 21
Pill · Cal Hacks 13.0 · pill-site.vercel.app
Three answers. Never a guess.
Seven rules, checked in order. A model writes the plain-English “why”; it never sets the verdict.
PASS
Plateau in the reference band and the 90° channel rose with it (P1).
NEEDS LAB
Light dropped with no rise at 90°, no plateau, or a plateau outside the band (N1–N3).
UNABLE TO VERIFY
No reference for this drug, a fault, or too little data (U1–U3).

7. Three answers · notes: It says three things only: PASS, NEEDS LAB, UNABLE TO VERIFY. Never a fourth. In the clinic app PASS means this tablet's curve matched the reference band; it does not mean right drug, right dose or contaminant-free.

8. Money flow · notes: How the money moves, and who decides. One tablet goes into the station; deterministic code turns the curve into one of three answers. The policy agent runs six clauses in code to get a ceiling; Claude may only return that ceiling or escalate. Passed, no recall, within the cap and a pharmacist’s tap releases the escrow to the supplier; Needs lab holds it and drafts a lab referral; Unable to verify escalates to a person. The money column is three Coinbase CDP server wallets, USDC on Base Sepolia, testnet, and escrow moves are simulated until we have a CDP key. Underneath, another clinic can buy a verdict over x402: the 402 is real today, the paid 200 is still pending. Claude and Gemini explain and read; code sets every verdict.

≤ 30 s
It waits for the inline reading to fall 6 %. After 30 s the screen prompts.
04
Dissolving
1–2 min
The pill fills, the curve falls, the 90° channel rises. Flat at 90° while blue falls: the amber moment.
05
Settling
20 s window
Flat for 20 s (|dA/dt| < ε) means plateau. No plateau by 180 s: timeout.
06
Verdict
7 rules
Seven rules, in order. The rule that fired is printed with the word.

11. Six phases · notes: The clinic app shows a run as six phases, played here against its simulated station. Lights off for three seconds: the box must be dark, or the lid is open and nothing runs. Ten seconds of clear water as the blank; it must be steady. Drop the tablet; it waits for the reading to fall six percent. Dissolving: the pill fills and the 90-degree channel should rise with it. Settling: twenty flat seconds is a plateau. Then the seven rules give the word.
