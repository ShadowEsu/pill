THE IDEA
03 / 16
Pill · Cal Hacks 13.0 · pill-site.vercel.app
Lot numbers can be copied. Dissolution cannot.
A recall lookup trusts the label. Pill measures the tablet itself: one tablet in 900 mL of water, a paddle at the same speed every run, two light sensors watching it dissolve.
CHANNEL A · INLINE
Blue light fades
A 465–475 nm LED shines across the cup. Riboflavin absorbs it as the tablet dissolves.
CHANNEL B · 90°
Green glow rises
A second sensor behind green plastic sees the riboflavin glow. Chalk clouds the water; it does not glow.
Fig 1 · The station, drawn in code for the website: cup, blue LED, two sensors, thermistor, stepper paddle, ESP32, Pi touchscreen.

3. The idea · notes: Why measurement beats lookup: the FDA found falsified product carrying a real lot number. Two channels: inline absorbance and 90° glow. The ESP32 measures; it decides nothing.

Pill
THREE ANSWERS
05 / 16
Pill · Cal Hacks 13.0 · pill-site.vercel.app
Three answers. Never a guess.
PASS
Plateau in the reference band and the glow rose with it (P1).
NEEDS LAB
Light dropped with no glow, no plateau, or a plateau outside the band (N1–N3).
UNABLE TO VERIFY
No reference for this drug, a fault, or too little data (U1–U3).
Seven rules, checked in order. A model writes the plain-English "why"; it never sets the verdict.

5. Three answers · notes: Three words only. PASS means this tablet's curve matched last night's riboflavin references on this box. It does not mean right drug, right dose or contaminant-free.

CLINIC MODE · THE PAYMENT GATE
06 / 16
Pill · Cal Hacks 13.0 · pill-site.vercel.app
The verdict holds the payment.
SANDBOX MONEY
A clinic samples one tablet from each delivery. The policy agent reads the verdict, the recall check and the spend cap, and proposes release, hold or escalate. A pharmacist taps.
P1
A release needs PASS, no recall record for the lot, amount within the spend cap, and the pharmacist’s tap.
P2
NEEDS LAB: hold, and draft the lab referral with the curve attached.
P3
UNABLE TO VERIFY: escalate to a person; no money moves.
P4
A recall hit holds the payment regardless of the verdict.
P5
Amount above the cap: escalate.
P6
Recall lookup unreachable: hold until a lookup succeeds or a human overrides.
NEEDS LAB → held
PASS + tap → released

6. Clinic mode · notes: policy.md, six clauses, quoted verbatim in the app. The model can only downgrade an outcome. Release always needs a person's tap. All money here is SANDBOX.

WHAT PASS DOES NOT MEAN
14 / 16
Pill · Cal Hacks 13.0 · pill-site.vercel.app
Pill screens tablets. It does not certify them.
PASS MEANS
The dissolution curve of this one tablet, on this box, was consistent with last night's riboflavin reference runs. No red flags found.
PASS DOES NOT MEAN
That the tablet is the right drug, the right dose, or free of contaminants. Pill has one reference drug today. Anything it cannot compare goes to a lab, by design.
Results charts on our site are marked as an illustration until the calibration runs are on the page.

14. What PASS does not mean · notes: Say the limits before a judge asks. One reference drug (riboflavin). Charts marked illustration until real runs land.
