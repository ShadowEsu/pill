THE IDEA
04 / 18
Pill · Cal Hacks 13.0 · pill-site.vercel.app
Lot numbers can be copied. Dissolution cannot.
A recall lookup trusts the label. Pill measures the tablet itself: one tablet in 900 mL of water, a paddle at the same speed every run, two light sensors watching it dissolve.
CHANNEL A · INLINE
Blue light fades
A 465–475 nm LED shines across the cup. Riboflavin absorbs it as the tablet dissolves.
CHANNEL B · 90°
Green glow rises
A second sensor behind green plastic sees riboflavin glow. Chalk clouds the water; it does not glow.
Fig 1 · the station, drawn in code for the site (station.js) and captured from pill-site.vercel.app: box, cup, paddle, blue LED, two sensors, ESP32, touchscreen.

4. The idea · notes: Riboflavin absorbs blue and glows green. Chalk only blocks light. Two channels must agree, so a cloudy cup cannot pass on its own; the ESP32 measures and decides nothing.

THREE ANSWERS
06 / 18
Pill · Cal Hacks 13.0 · pill-site.vercel.app
Three answers. Never a guess.
Seven rules, checked in order. A model writes the plain-English “why”; it never sets the verdict.
PASS
Plateau in the reference band and the glow rose with it (P1).
NEEDS LAB
Light dropped with no glow, no plateau, or a plateau outside the band (N1–N3).
UNABLE TO VERIFY
No reference for this drug, a fault, or too little data (U1–U3).

6. Three answers · notes: It says three things only: PASS, NEEDS LAB, UNABLE TO VERIFY. Never a fourth. PASS means this tablet's curve matched last night's riboflavin references on this box; it does not mean right drug, right dose or contaminant-free.

WHAT PASS DOES NOT MEAN
16 / 18
Pill · Cal Hacks 13.0 · pill-site.vercel.app
Pill screens tablets. It does not grade them.
PASS MEANS
The dissolution curve of this one tablet, on this box, was consistent with last night's riboflavin reference runs. No red flags found.
PASS DOES NOT MEAN
That the tablet is the right drug, the right dose, or free of contaminants. Pill has one reference drug today. Anything it cannot compare goes to a lab, by design.
Results charts on our site are marked as an illustration until the calibration runs are on the page.

16. What PASS does not mean · notes: Say the limits before a judge asks. One reference drug, riboflavin; anything else is UNABLE TO VERIFY, by design. We never call a medicine good or bad.
