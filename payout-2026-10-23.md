## What Payout is (one line)
Cheap solar heat nodes plus the public forecast warn outdoor workers hours before dangerous heat, and when the heat holds, a capped AI claims agent pays each enrolled worker automatically, with every decision logged where the worker, the funder and the insurer can all read it.

## The consensus fixes (all 18 agree on the first three)
1. **Cut flood. Heat only.** One peril, one index, one replayed event, one tamper refusal. The HC-SR04, pump, servo, fan are out. Buzzer stays as the stop-work alarm.
2. **Fix the sensor story.** Rent a DHT22 or BME280 from the booth at opening; DHT11 is ±2 °C and the trigger band is ~1 °C. Vented white shield, never bare on the breadboard under a lamp (reads 5 °C high). Thermistor in a black ping-pong ball = "globe proxy"; call the output "heat index with globe proxy", never WBGT. Print sensor error next to every reading.
3. **Nodes are evidence, not the oracle.** Trigger = median of ≥3 nodes (or 2-of-3) agreeing with Open-Meteo within 2–3 °C, rolling 3-hour mean with hysteresis. Missing node = unknown, never zero; stale data holds, never pays or denies. "Node confirms, station decides."
4. **Policy as a plain-text file the agent reads and quotes** (`policy.md`): threshold, 3 consecutive hours, flat payout, pool balance, exhaustion rule, max payouts/season, per-hour cap, "escalate to a human when…". The agent cites the clause in every log line; it never invents terms.
5. **Agent escalates, never silently denies.** Tamper flag → human queue with an appeal button and written reasons. Demo BOTH: the hand-warmer refusal AND the basis-risk case where nodes say danger, forecast says fine, and the agent pays anyway (the Kenyan farmer's test). Show a human override.
6. **REAL vs SIMULATED banner on every screen** and in the video: live nodes + forecast + agent = real; heat wave = replayed Ahmedabad May 2024 or Phoenix July 2023; wallets, pool, enrolment = simulated; "Solana devnet, play money" said out loud.
7. **Worker screen first, insurer dashboard second.** No login, local language, SMS-style: "Paid ₹750 today, because 43 °C for 3 days." Works on a $30 phone. Warnings and the stop-work alarm fire for everyone regardless of enrolment; never paywall the warning.
8. **Rails, honestly:** fiat (Capital One Nessie sandbox) is the default payee rail because SEWA's women got bank/UPI and the Kenyan farmers got M-Pesa. Solana devnet is the auditable, adjuster-free, cross-border settlement ledger that the NGO, insurer and funder can all read; the Solana judge persona only accepts it if the pool and trigger rule live in a program so nobody has to trust our server ("agent proposes, program enforces, agent cannot move funds alone"). If that is too much by Saturday noon, keep Solana as the signed decision-log anchor and say so.
9. **Wording (regulator):** never "insurance", "policy", "premium" on slides or site. Say "parametric payout pilot; simulated pool; not a licensed product; basis risk: the trigger can miss real losses." Pitch the customer as "a cheaper, denser trigger layer for SEWA / Blue Marble / Red Cross anticipatory action", not "we are the insurer".
10. **Offline:** Pi as its own access point (hostapd + dnsmasq); nodes talk to the Pi; Open-Meteo replay cached; devnet transactions pre-signed as fallback.
11. **Tonight's physics test:** hand-warmer on the globe thermistor: time the slew rate; tune the tamper rule (implausible rise, neighbour disagreement, forecast disagreement) to the measured number. The Hyphenate judge will heat two nodes slowly over four minutes and unplug the third; the rule must survive that.
12. **Name the community** (Anthropic): Central Valley farmworkers or Fruitvale day laborers for the live US story; Ahmedabad vendors for the replay. Call or message one org before Sunday; a hacker or mentor who has done outdoor work counts.

## The six attacks at the table (prepare 30-second answers)
1. "Your sensor is the insured's sensor. Why would Swiss Re accept it?" → It doesn't. The station/forecast decides; nodes confirm, add street-level resolution, and catch tamper. Basis risk shown on screen.
2. "DHT11 ±2 °C vs a 1 °C threshold." → DHT22/BME280, 3-node median, 3-hour rolling mean, hysteresis, error printed on screen.
3. "I'll heat two nodes slowly and unplug the third." → Unplugged = unknown, holds. Two heated nodes disagree with forecast by >3 °C → escalate to human, not pay, not deny. Show the log line and the clause.
4. "Who holds the pool, who is licensed, who can override?" → Simulated pool; pilot for an existing programme; human override queue; per-hour cap on the agent key.
5. "A vendor in Ahmedabad cannot spend USDC." → Correct: fiat/UPI/M-Pesa is the payee rail (Nessie sandbox here); the chain is the funder's audit trail and cross-border settlement.
6. "Show me the case where the heat was real and your agent still refused." → Show it, with the appeal path and the 'nodes override forecast and pay' case.

## Demo (90 s, video opens with the moment)
1. Three nodes on the table; station screen shows live heat index per node, forecast curve: "Danger at 13:40, 2 h 10 m from now". Buzzer stop-work alarm is armed for everyone.
2. **The moment:** judge presses a hand warmer on node A. A jumps; B, C and Open-Meteo do not. Agent log: "Node A +9.4 °C in 50 s; B/C 29 °C; forecast 30 °C. Clause 4.2: implausible slew → escalate. No payout." Human queue shows the case with an appeal button.
3. Replay Ahmedabad May 2024 at 60x on a simulated clock: index holds > threshold 3 h → agent pays 5 enrolled workers: Nessie transfers + devnet tx links; worker phone shows "Paid ₹750 today, because 43 °C for 3 days." Pool balance drops live.
4. Judge: "Why 3 hours, not 2?" → agent quotes clause 2.1 and the SEWA precedent.
5. Pull the Pi's uplink: nodes, alarm and worker screen keep working; forecast shows "cached 14 min ago".
